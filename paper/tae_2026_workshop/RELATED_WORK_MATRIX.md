## Novelty discipline

The paper must not claim that decorrelated probes are novel. The target claim is conditional on completed gates: equal-N survival, geometry prediction of ranking/calibration transfer, and causal held-out transport with interaction ablation. If any gate fails, the claim ladder is reduced explicitly.
