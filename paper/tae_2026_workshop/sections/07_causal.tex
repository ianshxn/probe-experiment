\section{Causal Counterfactual Transport}
The central test asks whether the factorial interaction is mechanistically required for purpose transport. Geometry was used first to freeze the layer rule: among eligible layers (held-out pooled purpose AUC $\geq 0.80$), L5 was the minimum $\|C\|/\|A\|$ layer and L16 the maximum. Only afterward did we intervene. For each held-out purpose family, raw-space $\hat A$, $\hat B$, and $\hat C$ were fit on training families; purpose transport used $-2p(\hat A+f\hat C)$, while the ablation used $-2p\hat A$. The intervention was applied to the residual stream and the model forward pass continued.

The direct candidate-logit endpoint failed its frozen purpose gate (accuracy/AUC $0.4757/0.4747$), so no behavioral steering claim is made. Our primary endpoint is internal: distance at downstream L24 between the intervened activation and the natural matched counterfactual prompt (same payload and format, opposite purpose). We define $C$ benefit as the A-only distance minus the full $A+fC$ distance. At L5 the benefit was $-0.000518$; at L16 it was $+0.007132$. The pre-specified L16-minus-L5 contrast was $+0.007650$ (complete-payload bootstrap 95\% CI $[+0.002978,+0.012349]$).

The result is heterogeneous but not driven by one family. Family-level L16-minus-L5 contrasts were $+0.01038$ (ff01), $+0.01425$ (ff02), $-0.00629$ (ff03), $+0.00933$ (ff04), and $+0.01020$ (ff05); four of five were positive. The exact two-sided five-unit sign test is $p=0.375$, and leave-one-family-out estimates range from $+0.00606$ to $+0.01102$, all positive. Thus the direction is robust to family deletion, but universal family-level sign consistency is not claimed.

As an interpretable descriptive scale, $C$ closed a mean fraction $-0.000016$ of the natural source-to-target gap at L5 and $+0.004166$ at L16; the paired fraction contrast was $+0.004182$ (95\% CI $[+0.001597,+0.006900]$). At L16, the correct-sign increment was positive for both benchmark ($+0.004343$) and casual ($+0.009921$) cells, whereas wrong-sign $C$ was negative for benchmark ($-0.009797$) and casual ($-0.012248$). Norm-matched A-only and coordinate-shuffled $C$ did not reproduce the correct-sign pattern. The 32 fixed random controls had L16-minus-L5 mean $-0.001825$, SD $0.001402$, descriptive standardized contrast $6.758$, and finite-bank statistic $1/33$; these are fixed-bank descriptive comparisons, not Gaussian significance tests.

We define selectivity as improvement toward the correct purpose target minus improvement toward the wrong-format target. At L16, full transport yielded selectivity $+0.018753$; at L5 it was $-0.003579$. This supports purpose-counterfactual selectivity at the interaction-heavy layer, while the failed L5 selectivity is retained as a limitation. The conclusion is therefore narrow: under controlled prompt-provided purpose and format contexts, a correctly signed format-conditioned interaction causally contributes to internal counterfactual transport at a preselected interaction-heavy layer. It does not establish spontaneous evaluation awareness, behavioral steering, or policy control.
