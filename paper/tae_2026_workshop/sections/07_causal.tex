\section{Causal Counterfactual Transport}
After geometry, we froze a layer-selection rule before inspecting intervention outcomes. Eligible layers require held-out pooled purpose AUC $\geq 0.80$ and exclude the first/last block; layer 5 is the lowest-interaction eligible layer and layer 16 the highest. Training purpose families estimate raw-space $\hat A$, $\hat B$, and $\hat C$. At an eligible layer, purpose transport uses $-2p(\hat A+f\hat C)$ and format transport uses $-2f(\hat B+p\hat C)$; main-effect-only ablations remove $C$.

The frozen protocol requires direct candidate-logit baseline validation before steering, randomized candidate mappings, raw-space strength calibration, zero-strength/sign controls, and fixed random directions. A one-H100 Llama-3.1-8B job (`51488273`) now runs this protocol independently of the user-owned 70B jobs. Its result is not yet available, so this section makes no causal claim. If the endpoint fails baseline validation, the analysis will be restricted to internal causal mediation.
