\section{Causal Counterfactual Transport}
After geometry, we froze a layer-selection rule before inspecting intervention outcomes. Eligible layers require held-out pooled purpose AUC $\geq 0.80$ and exclude the first/last block; for the 8B development artifact, layer 5 is the lowest-interaction eligible layer and layer 16 the highest. Training purpose families estimate raw-space $\hat A$, $\hat B$, and $\hat C$. At an eligible layer, purpose transport uses $-2p(\hat A+f\hat C)$ and format transport uses $-2f(\hat B+p\hat C)$; main-effect-only ablations remove $C$. The same rule is being applied independently to the exact cached 3.3-70B geometry, with selection frozen by job `51494932`.

The frozen protocol requires direct candidate-logit baseline validation before steering, randomized candidate mappings, raw-space strength calibration, zero-strength/sign controls, and fixed random directions. After access approval, the independent one-H100 Llama-3.1-8B baseline retry (`51515708`) completed on all 288 items. The frozen purpose endpoint was near chance (accuracy/AUC $0.4757/0.4747$), while the format endpoint reached $0.5694/0.5988$; because the predeclared minimum-accuracy threshold is $0.55$, transport job `51515710` correctly refused behavioral steering before modifying any held-out activation. This is an endpoint-validation failure, not a null intervention result. A first internal-mediation submission (`51520918`) failed before loading its protocol path; corrected retry `51521619` completed the frozen internal readout experiment on all five held-out purpose families. At the interaction-heavy layer 16, full purpose transport changed the downstream purpose projection by $-0.0796$ versus zero-strength, compared with $-0.0667$ for main-effect-only transport; full format transport changed the format projection by $-0.2004$ versus $-0.1985$. Off-target projection changes were approximately $10^{-3}$, while 32 fixed random controls were centered near zero. These are factor-selective internal mediation results, not behavioral steering. The exact cached 3.3-70B baseline and transport path (`51495134` / `51495235`) remains independently dependency-gated.

No behavioral causal transport figure or outcome table is reported in this version: the direct-logit endpoint gate failed, although the predeclared internal fallback now provides a held-out-family residual-transport result. The internal result supports mechanistic relevance of the factorial interaction at layer 16 but does not establish generated-behavior change. The exact cached 3.3-70B protocol is also dependency-gated. Reporting a behavioral transport effect before an independently validated endpoint passes would convert an engineering artifact into a causal claim.
