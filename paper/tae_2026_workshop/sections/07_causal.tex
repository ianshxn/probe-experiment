\section{Causal Counterfactual Transport}
After geometry, we froze a layer-selection rule before inspecting intervention outcomes. Eligible layers require held-out pooled purpose AUC $\geq 0.80$ and exclude the first/last block; for the 8B artifact, layer 5 is the lowest-interaction eligible layer and layer 16 the highest. Training purpose families estimate raw-space $\hat A$, $\hat B$, and $\hat C$. At an eligible layer, purpose transport uses $-2p(\hat A+f\hat C)$ and format transport uses $-2f(\hat B+p\hat C)$; main-effect-only ablations remove $\hat C$. Layer selection is frozen from geometry rather than chosen from intervention outcomes.

The frozen protocol requires direct candidate-logit baseline validation before behavioral steering, randomized candidate mappings, raw-space strength calibration, zero-strength/sign controls, and fixed random directions. The independent BF16 Llama-3.1-8B baseline on all 288 items again failed the predeclared purpose gate (accuracy/AUC $0.4757/0.4747$); the format endpoint was $0.5694/0.5988$. Behavioral transport was therefore refused. This is an endpoint-validation failure, not a null intervention result.

We instead tested internal counterfactual transport. In the final BF16 run, full purpose transport at L5 changed the downstream L24 purpose projection by $+0.001585$ relative to the main-effect-only ablation; at L16 the difference was $-0.012939$. The paired L16-minus-L5 contrast was $-0.014524$ with a five-family bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.017485,-0.012023]$. We do not interpret this projection readout alone as behavioral evidence.

The primary internal endpoint compares the continued-forward intervened activation with the natural matched purpose-counterfactual activation at L24. Mean raw distance to the natural target at L16 was $1.833561$ for full $A+fC$, $1.840693$ for $A$-only, and $1.850649$ for zero strength. The full-minus-$A$-only improvement was $+0.007132$ at L16 versus $-0.000518$ at L5; the complete-payload bootstrap for the paired difference gave 95\% interval $[+0.002978,+0.012349]$. Wrong-sign $C$ produced an incremental L16 effect of $-0.011023$, norm-matched $A$-only $+0.002779$, and coordinate-shuffled $C$ $-0.002543$. Across 32 fixed random orthogonal-$C$ controls, the L16-minus-L5 incremental-benefit mean was $-0.001825$ (SD $0.001402$), with no control at least as large as the observed $+0.007650$.

These results support internal causal mediation of a factorial interaction at the interaction-heavy layer, not generated-behavior change. The exact cached Llama-3.3-70B path remains dependency-gated, so no matched three-model claim is made.
