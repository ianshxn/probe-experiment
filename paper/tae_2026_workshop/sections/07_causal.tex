\section{Causal Counterfactual Transport}
After geometry, we froze a layer-selection rule before inspecting intervention outcomes. Eligible layers require held-out pooled purpose AUC $\geq 0.80$ and exclude the first/last block; layer 5 is the lowest-interaction eligible layer and layer 16 the highest. Training purpose families estimate raw-space $\hat A$, $\hat B$, and $\hat C$. At an eligible layer, purpose transport uses $-2p(\hat A+f\hat C)$ and format transport uses $-2f(\hat B+p\hat C)$; main-effect-only ablations remove $C$.

The frozen protocol required direct candidate-logit baseline validation before steering, randomized candidate mappings, raw-space strength calibration, zero-strength/sign controls, and fixed random directions. The independent one-H100 Llama-3.1-8B job (`51488273`) failed before model loading because the approved Hugging Face credential returned HTTP 403 for the gated exact revision. No baseline or steering outcome exists; this is a technical access failure, not evidence for or against the causal hypothesis. If an authorized exact checkpoint becomes available, the frozen protocol remains the only route to a causal claim.
