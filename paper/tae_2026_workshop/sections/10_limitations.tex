\section{Limitations}
This sprint uses an English controlled bank and does not establish multilingual or naturalistic generalization. The bank contains 288 rows forming 72 complete payload blocks, rather than the intended 80, with unequal family/topic coverage; this coverage gap must be explained before confirmation-grade claims. The development activation ledgers cover 32 transformer blocks for Llama-3.1-8B and 80 blocks for the 70B legacy curve; the exact 70B parent archives remain pending provenance validation. Format changes prompt wrappers and response instructions, so the factorial format effect is intentionally a measurement construct, not a pure semantic variable.

Model generation, training, and scale remain confounded in the 8B/70B comparison pending the exact Llama-3.1-70B archive. The 8B causal baseline now loads the exact revision, but its frozen direct-logit purpose endpoint is near chance; behavioral transport was therefore refused, while the predeclared internal-mediation fallback completed with factor-selective downstream projection effects at the interaction-heavy layer 16. Direct-logit endpoints can be wrapper- and tokenization-sensitive. We do not use old jProbe or migration-bundle results as evidence.

The natural matched counterfactual is an evaluation target, not an activation copied into the source run: the intervention adds a fitted residual-space delta and then continues the model forward. Accordingly, the endpoint establishes a downstream effect of the interaction-directed intervention, not an isolated path-specific mediation; residual-stream bypass and multiple-mediator interactions remain possible \citep{vaidyanathan2026mediators}. The target-distance metric is internal and descriptive, and the L16/L16 same-layer records are excluded because the runner captures the readout before applying the target-layer hook.
