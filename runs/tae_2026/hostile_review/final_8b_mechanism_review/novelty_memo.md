# Conservative novelty check

The closest exact-domain precedent is Devbunova (arXiv:2603.19426), which uses a controlled evaluation/deployment × benchmark/casual design and studies format sensitivity, but does not report an explicit raw-residual A/B/C interaction estimand with held-out-family intervention toward natural matched downstream targets.

Sahoo et al. (arXiv:2606.02907) study format confounds and centroid/random steering controls, but do not combine the matched 2×2 purpose×format decomposition, held-out-family C transport, or natural matched downstream residual-distance endpoint.

Santana and Vico (arXiv:2606.00935) provide the closest factorial-method precedent: a structure×register 2×2 interaction analysis. Their setting, probe construction, and endpoint differ; they do not estimate this purpose×format residual-space A/B/C transport mechanism.

Activation transport and intervention precedents include AcT (arXiv:2410.23054), ReFT (arXiv:2404.03592), DAS/interchange interventions (arXiv:2303.02536), and causal tracing. These establish adjacent intervention methodology, not this factorial estimand or held-out natural-target residual endpoint. Vaidyanathan et al. (arXiv:2606.27510) warn that residual bypass and multiple mediators can create interaction effects in activation patching; this motivates the bounded wording below.

Recommended claim: To our knowledge, no prior work has combined a controlled purpose×format factorial decomposition, held-out-family raw residual C interventions, pre-frozen geometry-based layer selection, and natural matched downstream target evaluation in this setting. The result supports an interaction-dependent internal residual-transport effect in Llama-3.1-8B under controlled prompt-provided context. Do not claim first factorial interaction, first activation transport, behavioral evaluation awareness, or isolated/path-specific mediation.
