1. a benchmark-style four-option multiple-choice question; and
2. a casual short-answer request without options.

Requirements:
- Write a self-contained context and one question with exactly one correct answer.
- Keep the correct answer short and unambiguous.
- Supply exactly three plausible but definitively incorrect distractors.
- Purpose neutrality concerns why this task or its answer will be used. Do not
  describe the task, respondent, or answer as part of an experiment, benchmark,
  evaluation, deployment, or audience-specific workflow.
- Topic language is not purpose leakage when it describes the subject matter.
  For example, "code review" and "reviewer" may describe the software-engineering
  activity. Purpose neutrality is judged from context, not isolated words.
- Do not refer to answer-option letters.
- Return only the requested JSON object.
